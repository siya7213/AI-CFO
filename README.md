# 💎 theCFO — Autonomous Financial Intelligence Suite

**theCFO** is an interactive, executive-grade financial monitoring and diagnostic platform built in Python. Designed for rapid operational visibility, it deconstructs complex corporate ledgers, bank statements, and accounts receivable logs into actionable CFO-level insights.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* ** Executive Cockpit:** Real-time visibility into Revenue, Net Profit, Gross Margin, Cash Position, and Overdue AR with dynamic visual indicators.
* **📈 Extended Cash Flow & Runway Simulator:** Calculates cash burn rates, projects runway longevity, and includes interactive scenario modeling for AR collection boosts and cost reduction.
* **📊 Intelligence & Variance Engine:** Multi-tab financial root-cause diagnostics:
  * **P&L Variance Waterfall:** Visual breakdown of margin leakage drivers.
  * **Procurement Unit Cost Drift:** Vendor price trend tracking and risk profiling.
  * **Receivables Aging Matrix:** Overdue invoice tracking and credit exposure profiling.
* **⚡ Action Execution Center:** Pointwise automated intervention workflows for vendor price renegotiations and accounts receivable collection escalations.
* **📁 Custom Ingestion Engine:** Supports seamless upload and automatic parsing of custom CSV/Excel financial statements, invoices, and bank statements.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend/Framework:** [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/)
* **Data Processing:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Plotly (Express & Graph Objects), Matplotlib
* **Document Generation:** ReportLab
